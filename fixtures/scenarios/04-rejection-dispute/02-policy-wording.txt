Clause 2.14 Pre-existing disease. A pre-existing disease means any condition that was diagnosed, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within 48 months before the first policy inception date.

Clause 3.1 Pre-existing disease waiting period. Expenses related to a pre-existing disease are not payable until 36 months of continuous coverage have elapsed since the first policy inception date.

Clause 9.2 Disclosure. The policy may be void if the proposer misrepresents or does not disclose a material fact that was known to the proposer when the proposal form was completed.
