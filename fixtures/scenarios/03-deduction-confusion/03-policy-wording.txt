Document: Example Health Policy Wording, version 3.0
Applies to policies issued or renewed on or after 1 April 2026.

Clause 4.1 Room rent. Room rent, boarding and nursing expenses are payable up to 1% of the sum insured per day of hospitalization. Any amount charged above this limit is not payable.

Clause 5.2 Co-payment. A co-payment of 10% applies to each admissible claim where the insured person was aged 61 years or above at the time of first entry into the policy.

Clause 7.3 Non-payable items. The items listed in Annexure I are not payable. Annexure I includes gloves, masks, sanitiser, and administrative charges.

Clause 12.1 Endorsements. An endorsement issued by the insurer forms part of the policy and prevails over the wording to the extent stated in the endorsement.
